Papyrus 2.1 completes PowerPoint support.

- Slides now draw charts, SmartArt as node text, WordArt, vertical text, EMF/WMF pictures, gradient and pattern fills, glow, soft edge, reflection and colour effects
- Find in file, an outline from slide titles, bookmarks, links and read-along highlighting work over slides
- Embedded fonts in a presentation are used where available
- Speaker notes in a deck are not shown, and EMF+ only pictures get a labelled placeholder

The initial PowerPoint support from 2.0 is now the full slide viewer described in the README.
