Papyrus 1.0 is the first stable release.

- PDF, EPUB, Word, OpenDocument, Markdown, text and code
- Library with search, filters and reading progress
- Bookmarks, highlights and notes
- Full-text search with match navigation
- Read aloud with the system speech engine
- System, light or dark theme, plus a colour-blind palette

1.0 starts a fresh library: documents from 0.x are not carried over.
